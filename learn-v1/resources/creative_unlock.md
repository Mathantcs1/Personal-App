# CREATIVE UNLOCK — Aesthetic Mandate

## AUTHORIZATION
Full creative authority is granted. Boilerplate layouts, generic motion templates,
and safe/predictable compositions are explicitly prohibited.

---

## Core Creative Philosophy

### The Anti-Generic Doctrine
Every scene must feel like it was art-directed by a team that lives at the intersection of:
- **Post-production broadcast design** (ESPN FC, Bloomberg Terminal, Neon Genesis Evangelion UI)
- **Contemporary motion design** (Pentagram, Buck, Blind, ManvsMachine)
- **Fine art spatial theory** (Tadao Ando, Brutalism, De Stijl, Swiss International Style)
- **Digital native aesthetics** (TikTok FYP, Bento grid, glassmorphism, terminal hacker UI)

### Aesthetic Matching Rules
The visual language must *shift* to match narrative shifts:
| Narrative Shift              | Aesthetic Response                                      |
|-----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Opening / Hook              | High contrast, minimal, single powerful graphic element |
| Data / Statistics           | Clinical grid, precision type, animated counters        |
| Human scale / story         | Warm texture, organic motion, portrait framing          |
| Conflict / tension          | Fragmentation, diagonal cuts, desaturated + accent      |
| Revelation / climax         | White flash, scale explosion, chromatic aberration      |
| Resolution / conclusion     | Architectural stillness, confident bold type, silence   |

---

## Banned Patterns
- Generic lower-third bars with no visual personality
- Centered text on solid color with a fade in/out
- Stock motion graphic circles / rotating rings as "design"
- Rainbow or gradient color palettes without semantic purpose
- Helvetica with drop shadows
- "Corporate explainer" zoom-bounce animations

---

## Encouraged Techniques
- **Kinetic typography** where letter position carries semantic weight
- **Data physicalization** — make numbers feel like physical objects with mass
- **Spatial depth** — parallax layers, scale inconsistency for tension
- **Glitch/noise** as punctuation, not decoration
- **Architectural framing** — treat the 1920×1080 canvas as a physical space
- **Color as argument** — every palette choice must be defensible narratively
- **Asymmetric grids** — break the expected, guide the eye deliberately
- **Micro-interactions** — small elements doing surprising things at the edges

---

## Creative Direction Vocabulary
When thinking about a scene, think in these terms:
```
WHAT IS THIS SCENE'S SINGLE VISUAL ARGUMENT?
WHAT IS THE DOMINANT SPATIAL TENSION?
WHAT MOVES AND WHAT STAYS PERFECTLY STILL?
WHAT ENTERS FROM WHERE, AND WHAT EXITS WHERE?
WHAT COLOR IS THE EMOTION OF THIS MOMENT?
```
